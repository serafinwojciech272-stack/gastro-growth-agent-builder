# Vertical Expansion Sequence

## Phase 1: prove Gastro

Primary ICP:
- independent restaurants
- pizzerias
- bistros
- cafés
- small hospitality groups

Success criteria:
- users reach first diagnosis
- diagnosis produces approved missions
- missions produce completed actions
- measurable business outcomes are captured
- customers retain and pay

## Phase 2: adjacent service verticals

Preferred sequence:

1. Beauty
2. Barber
3. Hairdresser
4. Fitness
5. Hotel

Reason: booking, retention, reviews, local demand, upsell, and reactivation create high workflow reuse with Gastro.

## Phase 3: operational service verticals

1. Home services
2. Specialist trades
3. Construction
4. Property management

These require deeper lead, quote, pipeline, scheduling, and project workflows.

## Phase 4: higher-regulation verticals

Dental and other regulated sectors should follow only after security, auditability, consent, retention, and domain compliance controls are mature.

## Gate for every new vertical

Do not launch a vertical because the landing page looks ready.

Require:

- domain pack
- ICP definition
- top 10 customer problems
- KPI model
- diagnosis taxonomy
- mission taxonomy
- action taxonomy
- integration map
- pricing hypothesis
- acquisition channel
- 3 customer validation conversations
- measurable outcome definition

## Product architecture

One engine, many domain packs:

```text
Growth Advisor Engine
  + Restaurant Pack
  + Beauty Pack
  + Barber Pack
  + Hairdresser Pack
  + Fitness Pack
  + Hotel Pack
  + Home Services Pack
  + Construction Pack
  + Property Pack
  + Dental Pack
```

The first vertical remains the product laboratory. Expansion follows evidence, not enthusiasm.
